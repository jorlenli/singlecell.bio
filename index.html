Technology we build

Single-cell proteomics by mass spectrometry.

A prenatal neuron is 7 to 10 µm across and holds about 50 pg of protein. There is no amplification step for proteins, so the sample preparation and the instrument have to work at that scale.

Algorithms we build

From sparse protein measurements to trajectories, integration and risk.

Trajectories inferred from protein

Developmental pseudotime from radial glia through intermediate progenitors to excitatory neurons, computed from the proteome, with co-expression modules along it.

Integration across samples and modalities

One embedding for 232,328 nuclei from 38 donors spanning thirteen years, and chromatin, expression and motif activity aligned per cell type.

Cells mapped back to tissue

Spatial niches, signed distance to the maternal–fetal interface, and which cell types neighbour which.

Genetic risk assigned to cells

Genome-wide association signals from more than 10,000 genomes scored in every cell of the atlas.

Spatial biology

Every cell kept in place.

Multiplexed protein imaging, sequencing-based whole-transcriptome mapping, and imaging-based transcriptomics, on whole tissue sections.
